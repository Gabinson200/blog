DFT Matrix — Left Half Gives the Right Half

Each cell is e−i2πkn/N drawn as a unit vector, colored by phase. Click a cell to toggle it as computed, or hold and drag to toggle several at once. The cell half a row away, column n + N/2, lights up in amber: marked = when k is even (an exact copy) and − when k is odd (the negation).
