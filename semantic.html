    How positions are computed
    • At build time every article is split into semantic chunks: headings always start a new chunk, and long sections are cut where neighbouring sentences drift apart in meaning. Each chunk is embedded with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dimensions), and the vectors are stored in semantic-index.json.
    • The concepts you type are embedded in your browser with the same model, which downloads once (about 23 MB) and is then cached. The example concepts are precomputed, so they plot without the download.
    • 1 or 2 concepts: each chunk gets its cosine similarity to the concept. 4 concepts: each axis is the direction between its two concepts (right − left, top − bottom) and each chunk is projected onto it.
    • Top-K: for each axis the chunks are sorted by relevance (highest similarity, or largest projection in either direction) and the top K values are averaged. When K is at its maximum, every article uses all of its chunks. Lower K moves each article toward its most relevant sections. The axes are scaled to cover every K, so articles visibly move as you drag the slider.
